## The rules this video follows

1. **Every noun and verb is shown.** If the narrator says bombs, tank, card, newspaper, clouds, rubble or railway, that thing is on screen within about 0.3s, and the verb is acted out (fly, explode, throw, circle, plant).
2. **Cut every 2–3 seconds.** About 60 shots in 154s. A new sentence nearly always means a new picture, and often a new place.
3. **Five kinds of shot, mixed constantly:** animated map, characters on a blank stage (gags), characters in an illustrated place, close-up "location" illustrations (palace, Kokura, intact city), and real archive photos or photoreal explosions.
4. **Characters go everywhere**, including standing on the map, and they **physically interact**: throw, drop, get blown away, point with a stick, jump.
5. **The map remembers.** Fires, mushrooms, arrows, tokens and flags persist into later shots, so the final map tells the whole story.
6. **Lists animate item by item**, on the exact word: cities, targets, waves of bombers.
7. **Dialogue skits** carry the middle third. Lines are short, alternate fast, and end in a punchline.
8. **Emotes** (!!, ?, sweat, angry face) carry reactions without words.
9. **Dates** go top-right whenever the timeline jumps.
