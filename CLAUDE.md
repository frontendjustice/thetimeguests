# thetimeguests: project context for Claude Code

YouTube history Shorts channel **@thetimeguest**: animated "why/how" history explainers (true stories that sound made up; WW2 so far, but the channel covers all of history) in the style of MapLore-type channels, but with original topics, scripts and art. Everything is code-drawn (maps, characters, props) and rendered by this repo's engine. Read `README.md` for the engine reference and `docs/reference-breakdown.md` for the style rules learned from a shot-by-shot study of a top channel.

## Setup in a fresh session

```bash
npm install                       # map data, geo libs, fonts
node src/cli.mjs projects/mincemeat --shots   # smoke test: one still per shot
```

- Playwright/Chromium and ffmpeg are expected to be preinstalled in the cloud container.
- Gemini is used for voice, transcription and video review. Requests go through `src/gemini.mjs` (curl, proxy-injected key).
- Renders (`projects/*/out/`) and caches (`projects/*/.cache/`) are **not** in git. Approved voice takes **are** committed (`projects/*/assets/voice/lineNN.wav`, referenced by `rawAudio`), so re-rendering any finished video costs zero API calls.

## Workflow (follow this order)

1. **Topic**: not already covered by MapLore; must be *visual* (every beat drawable).
2. **Script**: about 150–180 words (about 60s). Hook with a paradox in line 1, short skit mid-way, payoff, quiet close. **Fact-check every claim with web search before voicing.** Keep uncertain details vague ("decades later", "July"), and cut what can't be verified. Save as `projects/<name>/script.md`. Get the user's approval.
3. **Voice first** (`--audio`): writes `out/speech-report.md` and `out/narration-preview.m4a`. Send the preview to the user before building visuals.
   - Narrator: `gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview`, voice `Charon`, Switzerland/Mincemeat style string (it gives about 5.3 syl/s). Pin `voice.model`.
   - Character lines: `gemini-2.5-flash-preview-tts` with other voices (Orus, Puck, Algenib, Iapetus), with `captions: false`.
   - Merge narrator sentences into about 7–10 clips (quota!). Keep `direction` short. Long prompts get **read aloud**.
   - Use `tts` for respellings and spelled-out years; `say` stays the caption text.
   - Gate each clip: the transcript must match. Check suspicious words by cutting the audio and transcribing just that part (this caught a dropped "him").
   - When a clip passes: `node src/lock.mjs projects/<name> [lines]`, then commit.
4. **Art gallery**: render new props as full-res stills before using them in shots.
5. **Shots**: about 2.5s each, every noun or verb shown literally, action from frame 0, a different location per beat. Check with `--shots`, then the full render.
6. **Review the actual MP4**: frame sheets every 0.5s; loudness (effects about 0.5 dB over the voice, no spikes in pauses). Fix, re-render, re-check.
7. **Deliver**: video, thumbnail (separate one-shot project, 1080×1920, checked at feed size), title (short hook, under about 40 chars, plus `#history #didyouknow` and an era tag such as `#ww2`).

## Animation rules (learned from frame reviews; check every shot against these)

- **Caption band is off-limits.** Captions sit at y≈0.86. Key subjects (faces, the prop the line is about) must end above y≈0.80. On `spotlight` backgrounds raise the light pool with `bg.floor` (e.g. 0.7) and stand the subject on it.
- **Things rest on something.** Props sit on the floor, the light pool, a desk or the coal heap. Floating is only for thought bubbles, icons and pop-up labels.
- **No stray droplets.** Moods `worried`, `scared` and `sad` show one drop or tear when the mood starts, then stop (engine). Use `sweat: "loop"` only on purpose. Don't add a `sweat` emote on top of those moods. The `sweat` emote sits at the temple.
- **Long hand props lean back.** Shovels and the like lean over the holder's own shoulder. Check they never cross a neighbour's face. Keep characters who hold props or speak about 0.45+ apart.
- **No overlaps.** Text, ticks, stamps and cards never cover each other or a label: put marks beside or below. Characters' feet stay at y ≤ 0.93.
- **Frame 0 is never empty.** The subject is on screen at the cut (`at: -1` or `0`), and cue-timed extras add to it.
- **Open containers read as open.** A crate's lid leans behind it, not as a stray plank.
- **New kit item checklist.** Add its timed fields to `CUE_KEYS` in `src/timeline.mjs` (otherwise cue strings are silently ignored: the Garbo door and typewriter bug) and its sound in `src/audio.mjs`. Render a gallery still before use.
- **Sound after a cut.** Stamps and booms right after a cut stack with the transition whoosh. Measure (effects at most about 1 dB over the voice); use `sfx: "tick"`/`"whoosh"` or lower volume.

## Voice gotchas (learned)

- `gemini-3.8-flash-tts` reads the style prompt **aloud** before the line. Don't use it (in an emergency, trim the take at the pause before the line and say so).
- Per-model TTS quota is about 10/day, and failed or retried requests seem to count. The transcription models also have daily limits (about 20/day each), so the transcript gate can be blocked even when voice is not.
- A per-minute 429 waits 62s once (engine). A `PerDay` 429 means stop until the reset at 08:00 UTC.
- Character lines get `"sps": 0` (natural acted pace). The tempo nudge (up to 1.25×) blurred the final "l" of "coal" into "coat"; a project-level `sps` also applies to characters unless they set their own.
- A line ending in a question can swallow its last consonant. If the transcript misses it, re-voice once with `tts` ending in a full stop and a short `direction` such as "say coal clearly".
- Keep one model per role in a video: narrator on `gemini-3.1-flash-tts-preview`, characters on `gemini-2.5-flash-preview-tts`. Mix only with the user's approval.

## Hard limits and gotchas

- **Gemini free tier: about 10 TTS requests per model per day.** Every probe counts. Read the 429 body: `PerDay` means stop until reset. Never let the engine silently fall back to another model or to silence; the render warns "SILENT PLACEHOLDER", so don't ship that.
- **Image and music generation (Gemini/Lyria) has quota 0** on this key, so all art is code-drawn. Engine hooks exist (`bg.type: "image"` with `prompt`) if a paid key appears.
- **Wikimedia, archives, HuggingFace are blocked** by the network policy, so there are no archive photos and no offline speech recognition.
- Gemini video/audio reviews have hallucinated (e.g. "background music" that doesn't exist). Verify with measurements and frame sheets.
- Word timing: estimated, then snapped to real pauses of 0.1s or more (`alignedTimings`). Shorter "pauses" are consonants.
- Fonts: Bangers (captions, headlines), Gochi Hand (speech lines), Inter (UI). Don't add CSS that overrides `font-family` on SVG text.

## Git

- Work on the session's branch; commit and push after every meaningful step (the container is ephemeral).
- Commit messages end with the attribution lines the environment provides. Never put model names in commits.
